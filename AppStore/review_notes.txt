Shinkeisuijaku - Notes for App Review
A memory (concentration) card game for young children in the Kids category (age band: 5 and under). The UI is Japanese only; labels are quoted as shown, with English in [square brackets].

1. SCREEN RECORDING
A screen recording from a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached to the App Review Information. It starts with the first launch and shows: the first game starting without the home screen, clearing it, the home screen, the stamp book, a locked difficulty, the parental gate, and the purchase and restore of the In-App Purchase.
The app has no account registration, login, or user-generated content. It has one In-App Purchase (section 3).

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
An ad-free memory game for children aged 5 and under, to play alone or with a parent or grandparent. Children turn over cards to find pairs in two courses: "どうぶつ" [Animals] (the same picture) and "ひらがな" [Hiragana] (a hiragana letter and a picture, to get familiar with letters and words).
It does not pressure children: no timer, time limit, move or mistake count, score, or ranking. A wrong pair just turns back over, with no "X" mark or warning sound. The app has no sound or voice.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, account, credentials, or sample files are required.
First launch: until the first game is cleared, the app skips the home screen and starts a 6-pair "どうぶつ" [Animals] game right away. After the first clear, it starts from the home screen. (To reach home without clearing, tap "やめる" [Quit], then "やめる" [Quit] in the alert.)
1. Find all pairs. "ヒント" [Hint] shows all cards for about a second. The clear dialog "よくできました！" [Well done!] gives a stamp.
2. Tap "ホームにもどる" [Back to home]. On home, "あそぶ" [Play] starts the last game again and "スタンプちょう" [Stamp book] shows the stamps.
3. "やさしい" [Easy] (6 pairs) is free in both courses. "ふつう" [Normal] (8 pairs) and "むずかしい" [Hard] (10 pairs) need a one-time purchase.
In-App Purchase: product ID jp.shilokuma.Shinkeisuijaku.pass (Non-Consumable, "ぜんぶ遊べるパス" [All-access pass]). It unlocks Normal and Hard in both courses and their special stamps.
To test it, either:
  a. Home > under "ほかの あそびかた" [Other ways to play], tap "どうぶつ" [Animals] > tap the locked "ふつう" [Normal] > "おうちの方に あけてもらってね" [Ask a grown-up to unlock it] > "おうちの方へ" [For parents] > parental gate > "購入する" [Buy]. After the purchase, that game starts.
  b. Home > "おうちの方へ" [For parents] at the top right > parental gate > "購入する" [Buy] or "購入を復元" [Restore Purchases].
Parental gate: the "保護者の方へ" [For parents] dialog asks for the sum of a 2-digit and a 1-digit number shown on screen (e.g. "12 ＋ 7 ＝ ？", answer 19), entered with the number pad and "決定" [OK]. A wrong answer changes the question. The price, purchase, restore, and external links appear only after the gate. Children's screens never show the price.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None, except Apple's StoreKit for the price, purchase, and restore. The app makes no network requests of its own and works offline. No third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking. Play data (stamps, cached purchase state, last course and difficulty, first-clear flag) is stored only on the device.
The only external links are the privacy policy and the contact form on the "おうちの方へ" [For parents] screen, reachable only after the parental gate. They open in Safari; there is no in-app browser.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The features and content are the same in all regions. The app is distributed in Japan only, as it is Japanese only.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry, and no third-party protected material. All illustrations (animals, fruits, a star, and the stamps) are original artwork made for this app and drawn in code. Text uses the Hiragino fonts built into iOS; no fonts are bundled.
